Hey, I'm Swathi 👋

I'm a Product Designer from Hyderabad, India, currently working at Lexodd Hypernova Pvt. Ltd. — designing SaaS dashboards, operational workflows and data-rich interfaces, and trying to make dense, high-stakes information a little easier to act on.

Outside client work, I design independent concept case studies for domains I find genuinely interesting — right now that's AML compliance and supply-chain operations — and I'm partway through an MBA in IT & Fintech Management, because I like understanding the business side of what I design for.

Want to talk shop? Email me — or find me on LinkedIn, Behance and GitHub — all real, all linked below too.

Professional work

Currently, I'm a UI/UX & Visual Designer at Lexodd Hypernova Pvt. Ltd., designing scalable SaaS dashboards and chatbot interfaces with reusable component libraries, running usability testing on key flows, and working design-system governance into day-to-day Agile sprints with product and engineering.

Before that, I was a UI/UX Designer at Techboox, translating ambiguous business requirements into user flows, wireframes and polished UI for enterprise and consumer platforms — including a full onboarding and dashboard redesign focused on clearer navigation and product adoption.

I also spent time as a freelance UI/UX & Graphic Designer with The Digital Arm, designing an end-to-end mobile experience for an AR/VR product — onboarding, core interaction flows, errors, empty states, and the micro-interactions that make spatial interfaces feel considered.

Education — MBA (in progress), IT & Fintech Management & Analytics, Manipal University Jaipur (2025–2027); B.Sc. in Animation (Minor: Computer Science), Aditya Degree College.
Certifications — IBM Enterprise Design Thinking (Practitioner & Co-Creator); IxDF — AI for Designers, Mobile UX Design, Portfolio Strategy. Top 10% Global IxDF Learner, IxDF Social Impact Award.

Beyond the screen

I came into product design through an animation degree, which is probably why I still think in motion and sequence even when I'm designing something as static as a dashboard. That, plus a growing interest in fintech and compliance, is what pulled me toward the messy, data-heavy end of B2B SaaS — case management, risk, operations — where the real design problem is usually deciding what not to show someone.

I also host "UX With a Side of Swathi," a UX podcast on Spotify, and spend a fair amount of time on the Interaction Design Foundation — enough to land in their top 10% of global learners and pick up a Social Impact Award along the way.
